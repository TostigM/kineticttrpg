# Features

Features are AP purchases that grant **access** to equipment, abilities, or mechanical options. Unlike skills, features do not give a bonus to rolls — they unlock things you can do that you otherwise cannot.

Features are either:
- **Purchased** with AP at any point during advancement, or
- **Granted for free** by a species or background choice, in which case they work identically to purchased features with no AP cost

There are no ranks for features. You either have the feature or you don't.

---

## Armor

Without the relevant armor feature, wearing armor imposes penalties. The tiers must be purchased in order — you cannot purchase Medium Armor without first having Light Armor.

### Light Armor — 1 AP
**Unlocks:** Leather, padded, hide, and equivalent armor.

You can wear light armor without penalty. Without this feature, light armor imposes −2 to all Agility-based rolls.

*Referenced in: Human Tier 2 (Militia Trained)*

---

### Medium Armor — 2 AP
**Unlocks:** Chainmail, scale mail, breastplate, and equivalent armor.
**Prerequisite:** Light Armor

You can wear medium armor without penalty. Without this feature, medium armor imposes −3 to Agility rolls and −1 to all other rolls.

---

### Heavy Armor — 3 AP
**Unlocks:** Plate, full plate, and equivalent armor.
**Prerequisite:** Medium Armor

You can wear heavy armor without penalty. Without this feature, heavy armor imposes −4 to Agility rolls, −2 to all other rolls, and prevents the use of Quick Actions entirely.

---

## Shields

### Shield Use — 1 AP
**Unlocks:** Active use of a shield in combat.

You can use a shield to gain its listed defense bonus and use it as an active blocking tool. Without this feature, carrying a shield provides no mechanical benefit.

*Referenced in: Dwarf Tier 2 (Tunnel Fighter)*

## Magic Access

Magic in the Kinetic System comes in two types: **Arcane** and **Divine**. Both follow the same access structure but draw from separate spell lists. The two types are fully independent — levels purchased in one do not carry over to the other.

To use magic of any kind, you must first purchase the relevant Cantrip feature. Each subsequent spell level requires the previous level of the same type.

---

### How Magic Levels Work

Each level purchase grants **1 spell of your choice** from the spell list of that type, at any level **equal to or below** the level being purchased. When you buy Level 3 Arcane, you pick one spell of Level 1, 2, or 3 — your choice. The higher the level purchased, the more flexibility you have.

**A tier can be purchased multiple times.** Each additional purchase of the same tier costs the same AP and grants 1 additional spell at the same flexibility range. There is no cap on how many times a tier can be repurchased.

Cantrips are at-will and generate no Heat. Spells generate Heat based on their level (defined in the spell lists).

---

### Spell Budget (Casting Limits)

Casting spells costs from a pool of **Spell Points** that resets on rest. Each magic type (Arcane and Divine) has its own independent pool — a character with both manages two separate budgets.

**Spell Points per long rest = Spellcasting Stat**

| Magic Type | Spellcasting Stat |
|------------|-------------------|
| Arcane | Intellect |
| Divine | Will |

Each spell cast costs Spell Points equal to its level. Cantrips always cost 0 and are never limited by the budget.

| Spell Level | Spell Point Cost |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Cantrip | 0 (unlimited) |
| Level 1 | 1 |
| Level 2 | 2 |
| Level 3 | 3 |
| Level 4 | 4 |
| Level 5 | 5 |
| Level 6 | 6 |
| Level 7 | 7 |
| Level 8 | 8 |
| Level 9 | 9 |

**Short rest recovery:** Recover Spell Points equal to half your Spellcasting Stat (round up).

#### Examples

> **Intellect +4** → 4 Arcane Spell Points per long rest, recover 2 on short rest.
> Options: four Level 1 spells, or one Level 3 + one Level 1, or one Level 4 and done until rest.

> **Intellect +6** → 6 Arcane Spell Points per long rest, recover 3 on short rest.
> Options: one Level 6 spell (all in), two Level 3 spells, six Level 1 spells, or any mix totalling 6.

> **Intellect +4 and Will +3** (dual caster) → 4 Arcane + 3 Divine, each pool recovering independently.

#### Design Note
Pushing your spellcasting stat higher directly increases casting endurance. A character who dips into magic gets the spells but runs dry quickly. A dedicated caster who commits to Intellect or Will can sustain meaningful magical output across a full adventuring day.

## Senses

### Darkvision — 2 AP
**Unlocks:** Vision in darkness and dim light.

You see in dim light as though it were bright light, and in total darkness as though it were dim light, out to 60ft. You cannot discern color in darkness — only shades of gray.

*Referenced in: Old species documentation (Dwarf Underground, Gnome Burrow-folk). Not present in current species doc — available as a standalone purchase.*

## Combat Features

### Natural Armor — 2 AP
**Unlocks:** Inherent physical protection.

Your body provides passive damage reduction. Natural Armor 1 reduces all incoming physical damage by 1. Natural Armor from this feature stacks with worn armor up to a **combined maximum bonus of 2**.

> Species features that grant Natural Armor (Anthro Dense Hide, Orc Iron Hide, Dwarf Living Fortress) do not require this AP purchase — they are granted automatically. This entry is for characters who want Natural Armor outside their species grants.

*Referenced in: Anthro Tier 1 CON (Dense Hide), Orc Tier 4 (Iron Hide), Dwarf Tier 4 (Living Fortress)*

---

### Blood-Rage — 2 AP
**Unlocks:** A combat rage state.

Once per combat, you may enter a rage. While raging:
- +2 damage on all melee attacks
- You cannot use skills that require Concentration

The rage lasts until the end of combat or until you choose to end it as a free action on your Spotlight turn.

*Referenced in: Orc Tier 4 (Blood-Rage — granted as species Distinction)*

## Combat Maneuvers

Combat maneuvers are purchasable techniques that give characters access to specific fighting options beyond a standard attack. They require the relevant **weapon proficiency** as a prerequisite unless noted otherwise. Like all features, they unlock what you can do — they don't add a numeric bonus to rolls.

**AP Cost tiers:**
- **1 AP** — Passive improvements, minor reactions, simple additions
- **2 AP** — Active maneuvers, meaningful combat options, stronger triggered abilities

Each entry lists its **action cost** (Main Action, Quick Action, Passive, or Reaction), any **prerequisite**, and its **effect**.

---

### Heavy Weapons
*Prerequisite: Martial Weapons rank 1. Greataxe, warhammer, greatsword, maul, and similar two-handed melee weapons.*

**Cleave — 2 AP**
*Main Action.* When you hit a target, extend the swing to strike one additional adjacent target. Make a separate attack roll against the second target at −2. Both attacks share the single Main Action's Heat cost.

**Sweeping Strike — 2 AP**
*Main Action.* Swing in a wide arc targeting all creatures within melee range. Apply one attack roll to each target individually. Generates +1 additional Heat per target beyond the first.

**Crushing Blow — 2 AP**
*Main Action modifier.* Declare before rolling. Take −2 to the attack roll. On a hit, apply `[Brittle]` to the target in addition to normal damage.

**Overhead Smash — 2 AP**
*Main Action modifier.* Wind up for maximum force. +3 damage on a hit. Generates +2 additional Heat beyond the action's standard cost.

**Power Through — 1 AP**
*Passive.* When you deal damage with a melee hit, you may push the target up to 10ft in a straight line. If they contact a wall or solid obstacle, they take 1d4 additional damage.

**Reckless Assault — 1 AP**
*Main Action modifier.* Declare before rolling. +2 to your attack roll. After the attack resolves, the Definition may trigger one Reaction against you at 0 Heat cost.

---

### One-Handed Weapons
*Prerequisite: Simple Weapons or Martial Weapons rank 1. Longsword, mace, axe, morningstar, and similar single-handed melee weapons.*

**Riposte — 2 AP**
*Reaction.* When a Definition uses a Reaction targeting you and it misses or fails, immediately make one free attack against it. This attack generates no Heat.

**Disarm — 1 AP**
*Main Action.* Instead of dealing damage, attempt to knock a target's weapon free. Make an attack roll opposed by the target's Prowess. On success, the weapon lands 1d4×5ft away in a random direction and the target cannot use it again until they spend a Main Action to retrieve it.

**Pommel Strike — 1 AP**
*Quick Action.* Strike with the butt of your weapon for 1d4 damage and apply `[Dazed]`. Generates +1 Heat.

**Press the Advantage — 1 AP**
*Passive.* Whenever you score an Impactful Hit, take a free 5ft repositioning move that does not trigger Reactions.

**Parry — 2 AP**
*Reaction.* When targeted by a physical melee attack, declare Parry before damage is rolled. Reduce the incoming damage by your Prowess modifier.

---

### Finesse and Light Weapons
*Prerequisite: Martial Weapons (Finesse) rank 1 or Simple Weapons rank 1. Dagger, rapier, shortsword, hand crossbow.*

**Feint — 1 AP**
*Quick Action.* Misdirect your opponent's attention. Your next attack this Spotlight turn generates 1 fewer Heat Tick.

**Backstab — 2 AP**
*Passive.* When you attack a target that has any active Primer applied (by you or an ally), deal +2 damage on a hit.

**Flurry — 2 AP**
*Main Action.* Make two quick weapon attacks against the same target or two separate adjacent targets. Each attack rolls at −1. Both attacks together count as one Main Action for Heat generation.

**Vital Strike — 2 AP**
*Main Action modifier.* Declare before rolling. On a hit, your attack bypasses Natural Armor entirely. On an Impactful Hit, the target cannot use Reactions until the start of their next turn.

**Blade Weave — 1 AP**
*Passive.* After you hit a target this Spotlight turn, the next ally to attack that same target before the end of the round gains +1 to their roll.

---

### Reach Weapons
*Prerequisite: Martial Weapons rank 1. Spear, halberd, glaive, lance, pike.*

**Extended Reach — 1 AP**
*Passive.* You can make melee attacks against targets up to 10ft away instead of 5ft. Attacking targets within 5ft with a reach weapon imposes −2 to the roll.

**Set Against Charge — 1 AP**
*Passive.* If a target moves at least 15ft toward you and enters your reach, you may make one free attack against them before they act. On a hit, the attack deals double damage.

**Sweep the Legs — 2 AP**
*Main Action.* Attempt to trip rather than damage. Make an attack roll. On a success, the target is knocked prone and `[Dazed]` until the start of their next turn. Generates +2 Heat.

**Keep at Bay — 2 AP**
*Passive.* Enemies trying to close to within 5ft of you must make an Agility check (DC 13) or spend an additional Main Action to close the distance. Can be applied to each enemy once per encounter.

**Formation Strike — 1 AP**
*Passive.* +1 to your attack roll for each ally adjacent to you who is also wielding a reach weapon, up to a maximum of +3.

---

### Unarmed and Brawling
*Prerequisite: Unarmed Combat rank 1.*

**Grapple — 1 AP**
*Main Action.* Attempt to seize a target no more than one size larger than you. Make a Prowess check opposed by the target's choice of Agility or Prowess. On success, the target is **Grappled**: they cannot move and attacks against them by you and your allies gain +1. Maintaining the grapple costs a Quick Action each of your Spotlight turns.

**Haymaker — 1 AP**
*Main Action modifier.* Telegraph a massive punch. The target sees it coming and may spend a Reaction at 0 Heat cost to dodge. If the punch lands, apply `[Dazed]`. If it misses, you are off-balance: −1 to rolls until the start of your next Spotlight turn.

**Clinch — 2 AP**
*Passive.* While a target is Grappled by you, they cannot use Main Action skills that require significant movement or gestures (GM adjudicates). They may still attack you normally.

**Throw — 2 AP**
*Main Action. Requires: target is currently Grappled.* Hurl the grappled target up to 15ft. They take 1d6 damage and are knocked prone. If another creature occupies the landing zone, both targets must make a DC 13 Agility check or take the same damage and become `[Dazed]`.

**Body Block — 1 AP**
*Reaction.* When an ally within 5ft is targeted by a physical attack, step in front of it and take the hit in their place. The attack resolves fully against you.

---

### Bows and Crossbows
*Prerequisite: Simple Weapons or Martial Weapons rank 1.*

**Pinning Shot — 2 AP**
*Main Action.* Fire to pin a target to a surface rather than deal damage. On a hit, the target is `[Rooted]` — they cannot move until they spend a Main Action to pull free (or an ally spends a Quick Action to assist). Generates +2 Heat.

**Called Shot — 2 AP**
*Main Action modifier.* Aim for a specific location. Take −2 to the attack roll and choose one effect on hit:
- *Head:* Target is `[Dazed]`.
- *Legs:* Target's movement is halved until the end of their next turn.
- *Weapon arm:* Target takes −1 to attack rolls for the rest of the encounter.

**Suppressing Fire — 2 AP**
*Main Action.* Lay fire over a 10ft zone. Until the start of your next Spotlight turn, any enemy acting within the zone generates +1 additional Heat on their first action inside it. No attack roll required. Generates +3 Heat.

**Volley — 2 AP**
*Main Action.* Fire into a 10ft area rather than a single target. All creatures in the zone make a DC 13 Agility check or take damage. Generates +1 Heat per target beyond the first, +4 Heat total.

**Quick Draw — 1 AP**
*Passive.* You may draw and fire a ranged weapon as a Quick Action (0.5 budget) once per Spotlight turn. This attack generates 0 Heat.

**Trick Shot — 2 AP**
*Main Action.* Ricochet a shot off a surface to hit a target behind cover. This attack ignores all cover bonuses. Generates +3 Heat.

---

### Thrown Weapons
*Prerequisite: Simple Weapons or Basic Weapons rank 1.*

**Fan of Knives — 2 AP**
*Main Action.* Throw 2–3 light weapons at separate targets within 20ft simultaneously. Make one attack roll and apply it against each target. Generates +1 Heat per target beyond the first.

**Impact Throw — 1 AP**
*Main Action.* Hurl a heavy or improvised object. Deals 1d6 damage and applies `[Dazed]` on hit. Does not require a weapon proficiency — anything throwable qualifies.

**Boomerang Strike — 2 AP**
*Passive.* When a thrown weapon misses its target, it returns to your hand at the end of your Spotlight turn. When it hits, you may choose to have it return or embed in the target, applying `[Bleeding]`.

---

### Shield Maneuvers
*Prerequisite: Shield Use.*

**Shield Bash — 1 AP**
*Quick Action.* Strike with your shield for 1d4 damage and apply `[Dazed]`. Does not consume your Main Action.

**Shield Wall — 1 AP**
*Passive.* When you and at least one adjacent ally both have Shield Use, both of you gain +1 to all defense checks and Agility rolls against area effects.

**Deflect — 2 AP**
*Reaction.* When a Definition's Reaction or Interrupt targets you with damage, interpose your shield before damage is rolled. Reduce the damage by 1d6 + your Prowess modifier.

**Bull Rush — 2 AP**
*Main Action.* Charge up to 15ft in a straight line with your shield raised. Make a Prowess check (DC 13). On success, push the target back 10ft and knock them prone. Generates +3 Heat.

**Tower Defense — 1 AP**
*Main Action.* Plant your shield and brace. Until your next Spotlight turn, you and one adjacent ally gain full cover against ranged attacks. You cannot move while braced but may still make melee attacks.

**Rebuke — 2 AP**
*Passive.* When a Definition uses a Heat-costing ability that targets you and it fails or is negated, the Definition loses 1 Heat from its pool (minimum 0).

---

### Universal Combat Features
*No weapon or equipment prerequisite.*

**Combat Reflexes — 2 AP**
*Passive.* Once per encounter, you may take a personal reaction outside the Group Interrupt — reduce damage, negate a condition, or abort movement targeting you. This is in addition to your contribution to the Group Interrupt.

**Opportunist — 1 AP**
*Passive.* When you attack a target that has had a Primer applied by another character this round, +1 to your attack roll.

**Sentinel — 2 AP**
*Passive.* Any Heat-costing Reaction that specifically targets you costs the Definition 1 additional Heat.

**Battlefield Awareness — 1 AP**
*Passive.* You cannot be surprised. You always act in the first round of any encounter regardless of circumstances.

**Execute — 2 AP**
*Main Action modifier.* When a target is visibly near defeat (below roughly 25% HP, GM confirms), declare Execute before rolling. On a hit, the attack automatically triggers any active Detonator match as if it were an Impactful Hit, regardless of whether the Detonator tag is on your weapon.

**Coordinated Strike — 1 AP**
*Passive.* When you and an ally both attack the same target on the same round, the second attacker generates 1 fewer Heat Tick on their action.

## Utility Features

### Deployed Device — 3 AP
**Unlocks:** A clockwork companion.

You have constructed or obtained a clockwork companion — a familiar, drone, floating sensor, or mechanical arm. It can perform one assigned task per round (fetch, observe, carry, signal, etc.). It has **4 HP** and is destroyed if reduced to 0. It is repaired to full during any short rest.

The device cannot attack and does not participate in combat directly. It generates no Heat.

*Referenced in: Gnome Tier 4 (Deployed Device — granted as species Distinction)*

---

### Pocket Dimension — 3 AP
**Unlocks:** Extradimensional storage.

You have access to a small extradimensional storage space — constructed, inherited, or bound to you. It holds up to **50lbs** in a space no larger than a large chest. Retrieving or storing items is a **free action**. The space cannot hold living creatures.

*Referenced in: Gnome Tier 4 (Pocket Dimension — granted as species Distinction)*
