# Frontier Background

This file defines the 5-level branching tree for the Frontier origin, with mechanical benefits at each decision.

## Decision Keys

### Level 1
* `1` **Outpost:** +1 Constitution.
* `2` **Trail Town:** +1 Agility.

### Level 2
* `.1` **Pathfinder:** Expert Navigation and route plotting.
* `.2` **Stockade Guard:** Expert watch rotations and perimeter defense.

### Level 3
* `.1` **Surveyor:** +2 to checks to identify terrain hazards and stable routes.
* `.2` **Lookout:** +2 to checks to detect distant threats or weather shifts.

### Level 4
* `.1` **Rapid Camp:** Once per scene establish light cover for you and one adjacent ally until your next turn.
* `.2` **Drill Response:** Once per scene an ally within 30 feet may reposition up to 10 feet as a free reaction.

### Level 5
* `.1` **Steady Fire:** First successful ranged hit each combat gains +2 damage.
* `.2` **Cold Nerves:** Ignore the first fear or panic effect against you each combat.
