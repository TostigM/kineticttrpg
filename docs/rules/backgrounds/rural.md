# Rural Background

This file defines the 5-level branching tree for the Rural origin, with mechanical benefits at each decision.

## Decision Keys

### Level 1
* `1` **Farmstead:** +1 Constitution.
* `2` **Hinter Hamlet:** +1 Will.

### Level 2
* `.1` **Fieldcraft:** Expert Foraging and Husbandry.
* `.2` **Workbench:** Expert repair of simple tools and wagons; during downtime restore one worn mundane item.

### Level 3
* `.1` **Cooperative:** +2 to checks to coordinate labor, aid another, or organize villagers.
* `.2` **Weatherwise:** +2 to checks to predict weather and avoid natural travel hazards.

### Level 4
* `.1` **Holdfast:** Once per scene reduce incoming damage by 2.
* `.2` **Evacuation Runner:** Once per scene move one adjacent ally up to 10 feet as a reaction.

### Level 5
* `.1` **Harvest Bond:** The first support action each encounter grants the target +1 to their next roll.
* `.2` **Militia Drill:** First successful weapon hit each combat applies [Exposed].
