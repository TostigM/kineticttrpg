# Species Definitions

This document outlines the species available in the Kinetic System.

## PART 7: SPECIES DEFINITIONS (ORIGINS)

Species provide mechanical boons via branching decision trees. A player selects a Base Species, then makes two defining choices. Players may describe their character's physical appearance however they wish; the Definition purely dictates the mechanics.

### 1. Human

*   **Decision 1 (Origin):**
    *   **Urban:** +1 Intellect. Start with 2 extra Languages/Lore skills.
    *   **Frontier:** +1 Constitution. Expert Foraging/Survival.
*   **Decision 2 (Ambition):**
    *   **Specialized:** Gain 3 extra AP at Level 1.
    *   **Versatile:** Gain 2 extra Stat Points at Level 1.

### 2. Dwarf

*   **Decision 1 (Environment):**
    *   **Underground:** Darkvision 60ft. +2 to stone-based checks.
    *   **Surface:** +2 VIT vs Extreme Weather. Expert Navigation.
*   **Decision 2 (Bloodline):**
    *   **Deep-Kin:** Iron Resolve (+2 Will vs Mind-altering effects).
    *   **Hill-Kin:** Stout (+1 VIT. +5 to Maximum HP).

### 3. Elf

*   **Decision 1 (Dwelling):**
    *   **Sylvan:** +1 Agility. Ignore difficult terrain in forests/brush.
    *   **High Spire:** +1 Will. Start with 1 free basic Arcane skill.
*   **Decision 2 (Echoes):**
    *   **Ancient:** Reduce the first Heat generated each combat round by 1.
    *   **Nimble:** Quick Actions cost 0.25 instead of 0.5 (Once per turn).

### 4. Anthro (Modular Biology)

*   **Decision 1 (Diet):**
    *   **Herbivore:** 2x Healing from vegetation/potions; -50% healing from meat. +2 VIT.
    *   **Carnivore:** 2x Healing from meat/rations; -50% healing from vegetation. Expert Tracking.
    *   **Omnivore:** Standard healing. +1 Stat Point to any attribute.
*   **Decision 2 (Niche):**
    *   **Predator (Carnivore only):** +1 Agility. Scent Tracking.
    *   **Grazer (Herbivore only):** +1 Natural Armor (Passive Bulk).
    *   **Scavenger (Any):** Immune to ingested poisons and diseases.

### 5. Orc

*   **Decision 1 (Origin):**
    *   **Badlands:** +1 Prowess. Ignore difficult terrain in deserts and rocky areas.
    *   **Conquered City:** +1 Intellect. Gain proficiency in Intimidation and one other skill of your choice.
*   **Decision 2 (Heritage):**
    *   **Blood-rage:** Once per combat, enter a rage for 1 minute. While raging, you deal +2 damage with melee attacks, but you cannot use skills that require concentration.
    *   **Iron Hide:** You have a natural armor of 1. This does not stack with other sources of armor.

### 6. Halfkin

*   **Decision 1 (Origin):**
    *   **Green-meadow:** +1 Constitution. You are an expert at farming and brewing. You can create a nourishing meal for up to 6 people from simple ingredients.
    *   **River-folk:** +1 Agility. You can hold your breath for up to 15 minutes and you are an expert swimmer.
*   **Decision 2 (Spirit):**
    *   **Curious Spirit:** You have a natural talent for getting into and out of trouble. You gain a +2 bonus on all checks to avoid notice.
    *   **Stout Heart:** When an ally within 30 feet of you is reduced to 0 HP, you can use your reaction to move up to your speed towards them and stabilize them.

### 7. Gnome

*   **Decision 1 (Origin):**
    *   **Burrow-folk:** +1 Intellect. You have darkvision up to 60 feet and have a +2 bonus on all checks related to gems and precious metals.
    *   **Tinker-town:** +1 Agility. You are an expert tinkerer. You can create small clockwork devices that can perform simple tasks.
*   **Decision 2 (Mind):**
    *   **Arcane Mind:** You have a natural talent for arcane magic. You start with one free basic Arcane skill.
    *   **Illusionist's Mind:** You can cast the `Minor Illusion` cantrip at will.

### 8. Half-bloods

*   **Decision 1 (Dominant Species):**
    *   Choose one of the existing species as your dominant species. You gain one of the "Decision 1" options from that species.
*   **Decision 2 (Secondary Species):**
    *   Choose a second species. You gain one of the "Decision 2" options from that species.
