# Species Definitions

Species shape who your character is at a biological and cultural level. Each species has four tiers of decisions made at character creation. Players may describe their character's physical appearance however they wish; the tiers purely dictate mechanics.

## How Tiers Work

Each species has four tiers. Make one pick per tier, in order.

- **Tier 1 — Bloodline** *(Stat Tier)*: Pick one of five options, each keyed to a core attribute. Gain +1 to that attribute and a species-specific feature reflecting your innate nature and early life.
- **Tier 2 — Upbringing** *(Feature Tier)*: Pick one of five options. Gain a skill proficiency or minor mechanical feature reflecting how you were raised and trained. No stat boost.
- **Tier 3 — Identity** *(Stat Tier)*: Pick one of five options, again keyed to a core attribute. Gain +1 to that attribute and a more active, mature expression of that stat for your species.
- **Tier 4 — Distinction** *(Feature Tier)*: Pick one of five options. Gain a stronger feature that defines what makes your character exceptional. No stat boost.

**You may pick the same attribute on both stat tiers.** Doing so stacks the stat boost and gives you two different features from that attribute's column. Maximum stat grant from species: +2 total.

## 1. Human

Humans are defined by drive, adaptability, and the stubborn refusal to be outclassed.

### Tier 1 — Bloodline *(pick one)*

- **+1 Prowess** — *Workhorse*: Carrying capacity doubled. You do not suffer movement penalties from encumbrance.
- **+1 Agility** — *Street Runner*: You can move through spaces occupied by hostile creatures without provoking Reactions.
- **+1 Constitution** — *Hardened*: +4 Maximum HP. You recover 1 additional HP whenever you spend a Hit Die during a rest.
- **+1 Intellect** — *Quick Study*: Gain proficiency in two languages of your choice and one academic skill of your choice.
- **+1 Will** — *Stubborn*: Once per encounter, when you fail a roll by 3 or less, reroll it and take the new result.

### Tier 2 — Upbringing *(pick one)*

- *Traveled*: Proficiency in Navigation and Survival. You always know roughly where you are and how far you've come.
- *Tradecraft*: Proficiency in Persuasion and Appraisal. You know the fair value of most goods and negotiate without obvious tells.
- *Militia Trained*: Proficiency in Martial Weapons and Light Armor.
- *Lore Keeper*: Proficiency in History and Arcana. You can identify most common magical items on sight.
- *Field Medic*: Proficiency in Medicine. When you stabilize a downed ally, they regain 2 HP instead of 0.

### Tier 3 — Identity *(pick one)*

- **+1 Prowess** — *Relentless*: When you score an Impactful Hit, your next attack against the same target generates 1 fewer Heat Tick.
- **+1 Agility** — *Evasive Momentum*: When a Definition uses a Reaction against you triggered by your movement, you may continue moving after it resolves — it does not stop you.
- **+1 Constitution** — *Enduring*: Your Hit Die improves one step for free (d4→d6, d6→d8, d8→d10, d10→d12).
- **+1 Intellect** — *Tactical Eye*: Once per encounter as a Quick Action, ask the GM which Primer a target is currently most vulnerable to. The GM must answer truthfully.
- **+1 Will** — *Rally*: Once per encounter, when you pass the Spotlight, the receiving player gains +2 to their first roll instead of the standard Snap Pass +1.

### Tier 4 — Distinction *(pick one)*

- *Apex Generalist*: Gain 3 AP immediately.
- *Indomitable*: Once per session, when you would be reduced to 0 HP, remain at 1 HP instead.
- *Momentum*: Whenever you trigger an Impactful Hit, gain +1 to all rolls until the start of your next Spotlight turn.
- *Pressure Release*: When you use the Snap Pass to reduce the Heat Pool, reduce it by 2 instead of 1.
- *Polymath*: Gain proficiency in three skills of your choice from any category.

## 2. Dwarf

Dwarves are built from stone and stubbornness — enduring, precise, and deeply rooted in craft and tradition.

### Tier 1 — Bloodline *(pick one)*

- **+1 Prowess** — *Stoneshaper's Grip*: +2 to all checks involving stone, metal, or underground structures. You can identify the quality and approximate age of worked stone by touch.
- **+1 Agility** — *Sure-Footed*: You cannot be knocked prone by terrain effects. You ignore difficult terrain caused by rubble, uneven stone, or slopes.
- **+1 Constitution** — *Stout Build*: +8 Maximum HP. Your encumbrance threshold is calculated as if your Prowess were 2 higher.
- **+1 Intellect** — *Deep Lore*: Proficiency in History (Dwarven), Mining, and one additional craft or academic skill of your choice.
- **+1 Will** — *Iron Resolve*: +2 to Will checks against fear, mind-altering effects, and magical compulsions. You cannot be magically put to sleep.

### Tier 2 — Upbringing *(pick one)*

- *Forge Trained*: Proficiency in Smithing and one Craft skill. You can repair weapons and armor during a short rest with basic materials.
- *Tunnel Fighter*: Proficiency in Martial Weapons and Shields. +1 to all attack rolls made in enclosed spaces (tunnels, tight corridors).
- *Merchant Clan*: Proficiency in Persuasion and Appraisal. You always know the fair market value of metal goods and gemstones.
- *Runic Apprentice*: Proficiency in Arcana (Runes). Once per day, you may inscribe a basic runic cantrip effect on a surface or object.
- *Watchguard*: Proficiency in Perception and Investigation. You cannot be surprised while underground.

### Tier 3 — Identity *(pick one)*

- **+1 Prowess** — *Bulldozer*: When you make a melee attack as a Main Action, you may push the target up to 10ft. This push requires no roll but can be resisted by the target spending a Reaction.
- **+1 Agility** — *Mountain Dancer*: Once per Spotlight turn, reposition up to 15ft as a Quick Action at no Quick Action budget cost.
- **+1 Constitution** — *Unbreakable*: When a single hit would reduce you to below half HP, reduce that hit's damage by 3.
- **+1 Intellect** — *Structural Sense*: Once per encounter, identify a tactical weakness in the terrain or a constructed environment. The GM must reveal one exploitable feature (weak wall, climbable surface, collapse point, etc.).
- **+1 Will** — *Ancestral Defiance*: Once per encounter, when a Definition specifically targets you with a Heat-costing ability, reduce the Heat spent by 2 (minimum 1).

### Tier 4 — Distinction *(pick one)*

- *Living Fortress*: Natural armor 1. Stacks with worn armor up to a maximum combined bonus of 2.
- *Grudge-Keeper*: When the same Definition targets the same ally twice, you gain +2 to all rolls against that Definition for the rest of the encounter.
- *Berserker's Blood*: Once per combat, when you take damage, immediately make one free melee attack as a reaction. This attack generates no Heat.
- *Deep Endurance*: You require half the normal rest time to recover HP and remove conditions. You can go 48 hours without sleep before suffering fatigue effects.
- *Master Artisan*: Items you craft are Masterwork quality: weapons grant +1 to attack rolls, armor grants 1 additional natural armor, tools provide +1 to their associated skill checks.

## 3. Elf

Elves carry centuries of memory, grace, and arcane sensitivity. Their patience is a weapon.

### Tier 1 — Bloodline *(pick one)*

- **+1 Prowess** — *Arboreal Frame*: Treat falls as 10ft shorter. You can brace, hang, or climb using one hand without a roll for up to 1 minute.
- **+1 Agility** — *Fey Step*: Once per turn, when you spend a Quick Action to reposition, you leave no trace and pass through foliage, brush, or light obstacles freely.
- **+1 Constitution** — *Elven Resilience*: Immune to magical sleep. +2 to Constitution checks against poisons and disease.
- **+1 Intellect** — *Ancient Memory*: Proficiency in two ancient languages and Arcana. Once per day, ask the GM one historical or lore question and receive a truthful answer.
- **+1 Will** — *Timeless Bearing*: +1 to all Social rolls. You cannot be Charmed or Dazed except by abilities costing 5+ Heat.

### Tier 2 — Upbringing *(pick one)*

- *Sylvan Trained*: Proficiency in Survival and Stealth (natural environments). You leave no tracks in wilderness unless you choose to.
- *Arcane Schooled*: Proficiency in Arcana and one free basic Arcane skill.
- *Blade Dancer*: Proficiency in Martial Weapons (Finesse). You may draw and sheathe weapons as a free action.
- *Herbalist*: Proficiency in Medicine and Nature. You can identify any plant or creature from physical characteristics alone. You can create basic antitoxins from natural materials with 1 hour of work.
- *Lorekeeper*: Proficiency in History and two additional languages. You can read most ancient scripts without a roll.

### Tier 3 — Identity *(pick one)*

- **+1 Prowess** — *Fluid Combatant*: When you score an Impactful Hit, immediately take a free 10ft repositioning move. This costs no Quick Action.
- **+1 Agility** — *Blurred Motion*: The first Reaction a Definition spends against you each round costs 1 additional Heat.
- **+1 Constitution** — *Centuries of Endurance*: When spending Hit Dice during a rest, roll twice and take the higher result. You recover 1 additional HP from all healing sources.
- **+1 Intellect** — *Read the Weave*: At the start of each encounter, the GM tells you the Definition's starting Heat pool size and the cost of its cheapest ability.
- **+1 Will** — *Aura of Calm*: Once per encounter as a Quick Action, reduce the Heat Pool by 2 Ticks.

### Tier 4 — Distinction *(pick one)*

- *Heat Sink*: Reduce the first Heat generated each round by 1. Persistent passive.
- *Ghoststep*: Once per encounter, become undetectable for 1 full round. The Definition cannot spend Heat to specifically target you during this time.
- *Impeccable Reflexes*: Once per Spotlight turn, one Quick Action you take costs 0 instead of 0.5.
- *Eternal Vigil*: You enter a restful trance instead of sleeping and remain aware of your surroundings during trance. You cannot be surprised while resting.
- *Resonant Strike*: When you trigger an Impactful Hit by detonating a Primer, apply one additional Primer of your choice to the same target.

## 4. Anthro

Anthros are defined by biological specialization — predator, prey, or something in between. Their mechanics reflect an ecology, not a culture.

### Tier 1 — Bloodline *(pick one)*

- **+1 Prowess** — *Apex Predator*: Your natural weapons (claws, bite) count as Simple Weapons dealing 1d6 damage. Gain proficiency in Tracking.
- **+1 Agility** — *Prey Reflexes*: You cannot be ambushed. You always act in the first round of an encounter regardless of surprise. +1 to Initiative rolls.
- **+1 Constitution** — *Dense Hide*: Natural armor 1. You are unaffected by non-magical extreme weather (heat, cold, precipitation).
- **+1 Intellect** — *Pack Sense*: Within 30ft of an ally, you can communicate simple tactical intent without speaking (gesture, scent, signal). +1 to rolls made in direct coordination with that ally.
- **+1 Will** — *Territorial*: Once per encounter, designate a 20ft zone as your territory. Enemies entering it generate 1 additional Heat Tick on their first action within it.

### Tier 2 — Upbringing *(pick one)*

- *Scent Tracker*: Proficiency in Tracking and Perception (scent-based). You can identify individuals by scent and follow tracks up to 24 hours old.
- *Ambush Hunter*: Proficiency in Stealth. When you attack a target unaware of your presence, that attack generates 1 fewer Heat Tick.
- *Forager*: Proficiency in Survival and Nature. You can always find sufficient food and fresh water for yourself and up to 3 allies in wilderness environments.
- *Pack Leader*: Proficiency in Intimidation and Animal Handling. Non-hostile animals and beasts will not attack you unless commanded or provoked.
- *Adaptive Digestion*: Immune to ingested poisons and disease. You can gain nourishment from sources others cannot (spoiled food, raw meat, certain plants).

### Tier 3 — Identity *(pick one)*

- **+1 Prowess** — *Savage Momentum*: When you reduce a target to 0 HP, gain +1 to all rolls until the end of your next Spotlight turn.
- **+1 Agility** — *Scatter Dodge*: When a Definition uses an Interrupt targeting you, move 15ft as a free reaction before it resolves.
- **+1 Constitution** — *Regenerative Metabolism*: At the start of each of your Spotlight turns, if you are below half HP, recover 2 HP.
- **+1 Intellect** — *Behavioral Read*: Before a Definition acts, ask the GM what category of action it is most likely to take next (Reaction, Interrupt, or Spotlight Steal). The GM must answer truthfully.
- **+1 Will** — *Dominance Display*: Once per encounter as a Main Action, force a Definition to spend 2 additional Heat on its next action or forfeit it entirely. GM chooses which.

### Tier 4 — Distinction *(pick one)*

- *Apex Adaptation*: Choose one environment (arctic, desert, forest, underground, aquatic, urban). You suffer no penalties there and gain +1 to all checks made within it.
- *Berserk Vitality*: When reduced below half HP, you deal +1 damage on all attacks for the rest of the encounter.
- *Predator's Patience*: Once per encounter, delay your Spotlight turn. When you act, gain +2 to your first roll.
- *Pack Bond*: You and one designated ally (set during a rest) each reduce Heat generated by the other's actions by 1, once per round.
- *Scavenger's Fortune*: After any encounter, you always find one useful item (weapon, supply, material) in the environment. The GM determines what is appropriate.

## 5. Orc

Orcs are warriors and survivors — powerful, adaptable, and carrying the weight of a culture that the world keeps trying to break.

### Tier 1 — Bloodline *(pick one)*

- **+1 Prowess** — *Battle-Born*: Your unarmed strikes deal 1d6 damage. When you make a melee attack as a Main Action, reduce the Heat generated by 1.
- **+1 Agility** — *Warchaser*: Once per Spotlight turn as a Quick Action, close up to 20ft toward a target. This movement does not consume your normal movement budget.
- **+1 Constitution** — *Orcish Fortitude*: +6 Maximum HP. Once per encounter, when you are reduced to 0 HP, make a DC 14 Constitution check. On a success, drop to 1 HP instead.
- **+1 Intellect** — *Warlord's Instinct*: Once per encounter as a Quick Action, identify the highest-cost ability on a Definition's stat sheet. The GM reveals it.
- **+1 Will** — *Intimidating Presence*: Intimidation checks cost a Quick Action instead of a Main Action. Enemies that fail against your Intimidation effects generate 1 additional Heat Tick on their next action.

### Tier 2 — Upbringing *(pick one)*

- *Weaponmaster*: Proficiency in all Martial Weapons. Once per encounter when you draw a weapon, make one free Quick Action attack with it immediately.
- *Street Survivor*: Proficiency in Stealth and Deception. You always know how to find shelter and blend in, even in hostile urban environments.
- *Warband Scout*: Proficiency in Survival and Athletics. You move at full speed through difficult terrain.
- *Pit Fighter*: Proficiency in Unarmed Combat. Your unarmed strikes deal 1d8 damage and are treated as Martial Weapons.
- *Clan Negotiator*: Proficiency in Persuasion and Intimidation. You can communicate basic intent with any Orc or goblinoid without a shared language.

### Tier 3 — Identity *(pick one)*

- **+1 Prowess** — *Crusher*: When you trigger an Impactful Hit using a Detonator, deal an additional +2 damage.
- **+1 Agility** — *Battlefield Ghost*: Once per Spotlight turn, one 5ft move you make does not trigger Reactions or Interrupts.
- **+1 Constitution** — *Bloodlust*: When you take damage in combat, gain +1 to your next attack roll before the end of your next Spotlight turn. Does not stack.
- **+1 Intellect** — *Combat Adaptation*: From Round 2 onwards, gain +1 to all rolls against any Definition you have already attacked this encounter.
- **+1 Will** — *War Cry*: Once per encounter as a Main Action, shout a battle cry. All allies who have not yet taken their Spotlight turn this round gain +1 to their next roll.

### Tier 4 — Distinction *(pick one)*

- *Blood-Rage*: Once per combat, enter a rage. While raging: +2 damage on melee attacks, cannot use Concentration skills. Lasts until end of combat or you choose to end it.
- *Iron Hide*: Natural armor 1. Stacks with worn armor up to a maximum combined bonus of 2.
- *Unyielding*: Ignore the first source of knockback, difficult terrain, or forced movement each encounter.
- *Predator's Focus*: When you target the same enemy on consecutive Spotlight turns, your second attack generates 1 fewer Heat Tick.
- *Warchief's Authority*: Once per session, your presence can end a non-combat confrontation on your terms or trigger immediate combat — your choice. NPCs must comply or face consequences.

## 6. Halfkin

Small, clever, and routinely underestimated. Halfkin have survived by being faster, luckier, and more community-minded than everyone around them.

### Tier 1 — Bloodline *(pick one)*

- **+1 Prowess** — *Deceptive Strength*: You deal damage as if one size larger. The first time you attack in any encounter, that attack generates 1 fewer Heat Tick — enemies never expect it.
- **+1 Agility** — *Small and Slippery*: You can move through spaces occupied by Large or larger creatures freely. +2 to all checks to escape grapples, restraints, or tight spaces.
- **+1 Constitution** — *Comfort-Bred Resilience*: +4 Maximum HP. Recover 1 additional HP per Hit Die spent during rests.
- **+1 Intellect** — *Homebody Wisdom*: Proficiency in Lore (Local) for any region you have spent at least 1 week in. You always find someone willing to talk within 1 hour of arriving in a new settlement.
- **+1 Will** — *Halfkin Luck*: Once per session, when you roll a critical failure (1-1-1), reroll all three dice and take the new result.

### Tier 2 — Upbringing *(pick one)*

- *River Swimmer*: Proficiency in Athletics (Swimming). You can hold your breath for 15 minutes and swim at full speed against currents.
- *Farmstead Raised*: Proficiency in Animal Handling and Cooking. You can prepare a nourishing meal for 6 people from simple ingredients.
- *Burglar's Knack*: Proficiency in Stealth and Sleight of Hand. +2 to checks involving picking locks or concealing objects on your person.
- *Curious Wanderer*: Proficiency in Navigation and Survival. You are never truly lost and can retrace any path you have previously walked.
- *Community Voice*: Proficiency in Persuasion and Performance. First social interactions with strangers default to Friendly rather than Neutral disposition.

### Tier 3 — Identity *(pick one)*

- **+1 Prowess** — *Gutsy Brawler*: When you score an Impactful Hit on a target larger than yourself, they are off-balance — their next action generates 1 additional Heat Tick.
- **+1 Agility** — *Nimble Escape*: Once per encounter, as a free reaction when targeted by a Reaction or Interrupt, move 10ft out of range before it resolves.
- **+1 Constitution** — *Stout Heart*: When an ally within 30ft drops to 0 HP, you may use your reaction to move to them and stabilize them. No roll required.
- **+1 Intellect** — *Read the Room*: In social encounters, once per scene, ask the GM what the NPC Definition most wants from this interaction. The GM must answer honestly.
- **+1 Will** — *Unflinching*: You cannot be Frightened. When an ally within 30ft is Frightened or Dazed, spend a Quick Action to remove the condition from them.

### Tier 4 — Distinction *(pick one)*

- *Ghost of the Crowd*: In populated areas you are functionally invisible when not actively threatening anyone. Enemies do not identify you as a threat unless you have attacked them.
- *Rally*: Once per encounter, when you pass the Spotlight with a Quick Action setup, grant the receiving player +2 to their first roll instead of the standard bonus.
- *Underdog's Fury*: When you are the last player to act before a Definition's Spotlight Steal, make one free Quick Action attack against the Definition before it takes its turn.
- *Second Wind*: Once per encounter as a Quick Action, recover HP equal to your Hit Die result + Constitution modifier.
- *Fortune's Favor*: Once per session, declare Fortune's Favor before any roll. That roll cannot be a critical failure — any 1-1-1 result is treated as a 13 instead.

## 7. Gnome

Gnomes are inventive, sharp-minded, and perpetually convinced that the problem just needs a more interesting solution.

### Tier 1 — Bloodline *(pick one)*

- **+1 Prowess** — *Extended Reach*: Through gadgetry or clever leverage, you interact with objects up to 10ft away as if adjacent. You count as one size larger when operating heavy machinery.
- **+1 Agility** — *Clockwork Reflexes*: Once per encounter, negate a Reaction triggered by your movement. The Reaction is wasted without effect.
- **+1 Constitution** — *Compact Efficiency*: You require half normal rations. Environmental hazards (heat, cold, pressure, altitude) affect you at half severity.
- **+1 Intellect** — *Prodigy*: Proficiency in Arcana, Engineering, and one additional academic skill of your choice.
- **+1 Will** — *Illusionist's Instinct*: You can cast Minor Illusion at will (no Heat generated). The illusion fits within a 5ft cube and has no sound component.

### Tier 2 — Upbringing *(pick one)*

- *Tinkerer*: Proficiency in Engineering and Thieves' Tools. You can construct small mechanical devices that perform one simple task (open a door, carry a small load, make a noise on command).
- *Alchemist's Apprentice*: Proficiency in Alchemy. You can identify potions, poisons, and alchemical substances on sight. You can create basic alchemical items in 1 hour with materials.
- *Arcane Scholar*: Proficiency in Arcana and one free basic Arcane skill. You can identify any spell or magical effect within 30ft as a Quick Action.
- *Gemcutter*: Proficiency in Appraisal and Crafting (Gems). You can cut and set gemstones to enhance magical focuses or create minor magical components.
- *Saboteur*: Proficiency in Stealth and Engineering. You can disable mechanical traps or locks as a Quick Action instead of a Main Action.

### Tier 3 — Identity *(pick one)*

- **+1 Prowess** — *Overclock*: Once per encounter, a prepared device or weapon performs at double potency (double damage dice, doubled effect radius, etc.). After use, it requires 1 minute to reset.
- **+1 Agility** — *Escape Artist*: You can fit through any gap a creature half your size could pass through. You cannot be grappled by creatures more than two sizes larger than you.
- **+1 Constitution** — *Spark Resistance*: Immune to damage from electricity and small explosions. You can handle volatile materials without risk of accidental detonation.
- **+1 Intellect** — *Pattern Recognition*: Once per encounter, after a Definition acts, predict its next action type (Reaction, Interrupt, or Spotlight Steal). The GM must confirm or deny truthfully.
- **+1 Will** — *Mental Firewall*: +3 to Will checks against arcane mind control, illusions, and magical deception. You always detect when you are inside an illusion by spending a Quick Action to examine it.

### Tier 4 — Distinction *(pick one)*

- *Arcane Breakthrough*: Gain access to one Arcane skill chain's base skill without prerequisites. Skills from that chain cost 1 fewer AP to purchase.
- *Deployed Device*: You have a clockwork companion (familiar, drone, or floating sensor) that performs one task per round. It has 4 HP and is repaired during a short rest.
- *Eureka*: Once per session, when faced with a problem that appears mechanically impossible, declare a Eureka moment. The GM must offer one unconventional solution using your available tools and skills.
- *Overclocker's Push*: Once per encounter, for one Spotlight turn treat your Agility and Prowess as 2 higher. After this turn, generate 2 automatic Heat Ticks.
- *Pocket Dimension*: You have a constructed extradimensional storage space holding up to 50lbs in a chest-sized volume. Retrieving items is a free action.

## 8. Half-Bloods

Half-bloods inherit from two species. Rather than a fixed tier table, they draw from two existing species trees — alternating by tier.

### How It Works

At character creation, choose a **Dominant Species** and a **Secondary Species**. You may not choose the same species twice.

- **Tier 1 (Bloodline)** and **Tier 2 (Upbringing)**: Use the Dominant species' tier tables.
- **Tier 3 (Identity)** and **Tier 4 (Distinction)**: Use the Secondary species' tier tables.

All normal rules apply — you may pick the same stat on both stat tiers, and the features come from whichever species owns that tier.

Half-bloods do not get a separate identity; they get two. The Dominant species shapes who they were. The Secondary species shapes who they became.
