# Character Creation

This document outlines the rules for creating a character in the Kinetic System.

## PART 6: CHARACTER DEFINITIONS (BUILDING A HERO)

Classes do not exist. Characters are built using an open-ended purchase system.

### The 5 Core Attributes

*   **Prowess (STR):** Physical force, melee impact, athletics.
*   **Agility (DEX):** Speed, precision, evasion, ranged accuracy.
*   **Constitution (VIT):** Health scaling, resilience, stamina.
*   **Intellect (INT):** Logic, arcane knowledge, investigation.
*   **Will (CHA):** Resolve, charisma, mental defense.

### Point-Buy Scaling Economy

Players build stats from a starting pool. Costs escalate to prevent early min-maxing and establish soft caps.

*   **Negative Flaws:** Taking a -1 to -3 in a stat adds +1 point to your pool per negative rank.
*   **Rank +1 to +4:** Costs 1 Point per rank.
*   **Rank +5 to +6:** Costs 2 Points per rank.
*   **Rank +7 to +8:** Costs 3 Points per rank.
*   **Rank +9 or higher:** Costs 4 Points per rank.

### Level-Up Economy: Ascension Points (AP)

Instead of linear class levels, players earn AP to purchase upgrades.

*   **Skill Chains:** Buy individual skills. Skills have Prerequisites (e.g., Prowess 4, Agility 2) instead of class requirements.
*   **Vitality Upgrades (Hit Dice):**
    *   Every character gets a d4 + CON Hit Die per level for free.
    *   Spend 1 AP: Upgrade to d6.
    *   Spend 3 AP: Upgrade to d8.
    *   Spend 6 AP: Upgrade to d10.
    *   Spend 10 AP: Upgrade to d12.
*   **Attribute Ascension:** Spend high amounts of AP (8-12) to bypass the Point-Buy scale and permanently increase a Core Attribute.

### Campaign Power Tiers

The GM selects a tier at Session 0 to determine the starting economy:

*   **Basic (Gritty):** 10 Stat Points / 2 Starting AP.
*   **Heroic (Classic):** 16 Stat Points / 5 Starting AP.
*   **Super Heroic (Epic):** 24 Stat Points / 12 Starting AP.
*   **Legendary (Mythic):** 32 Stat Points / 20 Starting AP.
