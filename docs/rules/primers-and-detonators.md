# Primers and Detonators

This document outlines the Primer and Detonator system in the Kinetic System.

## The Synergy Loop (Tags)

To eliminate math-heavy modifiers (+1s and -2s), teamwork is executed through a keyword-matching circuit called the Synergy Loop.

### Primers (Tags Appliable)

Skills that apply status effects to a target, creating a vulnerability. A Primer does nothing immediately (or causes minor passive effects) until Detonated.

**Known Primers:**

*   `[Brittle]`
*   `[Chilled]`
*   `[Exposed]`
*   `[Bleeding]`
*   `[Dazed]`

### Detonators (Tags Detonatable)

Skills that trigger specific Primers for massive, automatic effects. If a player hits a target with a Detonator that matches the target's Primer (e.g., hitting a `[Brittle]` target with a `[Shatter]` skill), it triggers an "Impactful Hit." This bypasses armor/defense and yields maximum damage or narrative success.

**Known Detonators:**

*   `[Shatter]`
*   `[Gouge]`
*   `[Crush]`
*   `[Fear]`

### Multifunctional Tags

Tags like `[Force]` or `[Unstable]` that can act as either a Primer for a complex chain or a Detonator for a basic one.
