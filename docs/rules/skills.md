# Skills

This document catalogs all skills in the Kinetic System. There are two distinct types:

- **Proficiencies** — Broad competencies representing training and knowledge. Rolled as 3d8 + relevant stat. Without proficiency, you roll straight (no bonus). Purchased and ranked up with Ascension Points (AP).
- **Skill Chains** — Named active abilities purchased with AP. Trees of individual techniques, powers, and spells with stat prerequisites. See [core-mechanics.md](core-mechanics.md) for the Skill Anatomy format.

---

## The Skill Rank System

Every proficiency works on the same ranking economy.

**Without proficiency:** Roll 3d8 + relevant stat. No bonus.

**Purchasing rank 1** costs **1 AP** and grants **+1 to all rolls** with that skill.

**Advancing to the next rank** costs AP equal to the new rank:

| Rank | AP Cost to Reach | Bonus to Rolls | Total AP Invested |
|------|-----------------|----------------|-------------------|
| 1    | 1 AP            | +1             | 1 AP              |
| 2    | 2 AP            | +2             | 3 AP              |
| 3    | 3 AP            | +3             | 6 AP              |
| 4    | 4 AP            | +4             | 10 AP             |
| 5    | 5 AP            | +5             | 15 AP             |

Proficiencies granted by species or background start at **Rank 1 for free**. They can still be advanced further by spending AP.

## Weapon Proficiencies

Weapons are grouped into three tiers by complexity and lethality. Each tier has a different AP cost to reach Rank 1. Rank advancement beyond 1 follows the same formula as all skills (new rank = AP cost).

| Rank       | Basic Weapons | Simple Weapons | Martial Weapons |
|------------|--------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Rank 1** | 1 AP         | 2 AP           | 3 AP            |
| **Rank 2** | +2 AP (3 total) | +2 AP (4 total) | +2 AP (5 total) |
| **Rank 3** | +3 AP (6 total) | +3 AP (7 total) | +3 AP (8 total) |

Weapon rank adds **+1 to attack rolls** per rank with weapons of that category.

### Basic Weapons
Low-skill arms requiring minimal training. Available everywhere.

*Examples: club, staff, knife, sling, torch, improvised weapon, thrown rock*

### Simple Weapons
Common arms with some training required. Standard equipment for militia and travelers.

*Examples: dagger, handaxe, mace, shortbow, shortsword, spear, crossbow (light)*

### Martial Weapons
Dedicated combat arms requiring formal training to use effectively.

*Examples: longsword, warhammer, greataxe, two-handed sword, war pick, longbow, crossbow (heavy), lance*

### Exotic Weapons
Rare, unusual, or culturally specific weapons that require specialized instruction. These are gained as individual proficiencies rather than a category — each exotic weapon is its own entry.

*Examples: war-scythe, bladed chain, repeating crossbow, greatbow*

> **Note:** Weapon proficiencies granted by backgrounds (e.g., "Simple Weapons," "Martial Weapons") start at Rank 1 for free and can be advanced with AP.

## Proficiency List

Listed alphabetically. Each entry notes its **category** and the **primary stat** used when rolling.

> **Note:** Armor (Light, Medium, Heavy), Shield Use, and Magic Access (Cantrips, Arcane Skill, Arcane Chain Access) are **Features**, not proficiencies. They are purchased with AP but grant access rather than a roll bonus. See [features.md](features.md).

---

### Alchemy
**Category:** Craft | **Stat:** Intellect

Identifying, creating, and handling alchemical substances — potions, poisons, reagents, and volatile compounds. Covers both recognition on sight and production from raw materials.

---

### Animal Handling
**Category:** Social | **Stat:** Will

Calming, commanding, and reading the intent of animals and beasts. Covers domesticated animals, mounts, and non-hostile wildlife.

---

### Appraisal
**Category:** Knowledge | **Stat:** Intellect

Assessing the quality, authenticity, and market value of goods — weapons, armor, gems, art objects, and trade commodities.

---

### Arcana
**Category:** Knowledge | **Stat:** Intellect

Knowledge of magical theory, spell identification, runic systems, and arcane history. Covers identifying magical effects, reading arcane script, and understanding the Weave.

> **Subspecialty — Arcana (Runes):** Focused on inscribing, deciphering, and activating runic magic specifically.

---

### Athletics
**Category:** Physical | **Stat:** Prowess

Tests of raw physical capability — climbing, jumping, swimming, pushing through obstacles, and feats of strength. Used in both combat and exploration.

> **Subspecialty — Athletics (Swimming):** Extended breath control, open-water navigation, and swimming against currents.

---

### Cooking
**Category:** Craft | **Stat:** Constitution

Preparing nourishing meals from available ingredients. Covers field cooking, rationing efficiency, and creating food with restorative properties.

---

### Crafting
**Category:** Craft | **Stat:** Intellect or Prowess

Constructing, repairing, and modifying physical objects. Covers a wide range of materials and techniques.

> **Subspecialty — Crafting (Gems):** Cutting, setting, and enhancing gemstones for use in magical focuses or decorative items.

---

### Deception
**Category:** Social | **Stat:** Will

Convincing others of false information, maintaining cover identities, and concealing true intentions. Covers lying, misdirection, and forgery.

---

### Engineering
**Category:** Knowledge / Craft | **Stat:** Intellect

Designing, building, and understanding mechanical systems — clockwork devices, siege equipment, structural architecture, and traps.

---

### History
**Category:** Knowledge | **Stat:** Intellect

Recall and analysis of past events, cultures, conflicts, and notable figures. Covers both general world history and specific civilizational knowledge.

> **Subspecialty — History (Dwarven):** Deep knowledge of Dwarven lineages, clan politics, underground kingdoms, and stone-craft traditions.

---

### Insight
**Category:** Social | **Stat:** Will

Reading people — detecting lies, sensing emotional states, identifying motivations, and understanding what someone is not saying.

---

### Intimidation
**Category:** Social | **Stat:** Will or Prowess

Compelling cooperation through fear, implied violence, or sheer force of presence. Covers both subtle menace and direct threats.

---

### Investigation
**Category:** Knowledge | **Stat:** Intellect

Active searching and deductive reasoning — examining crime scenes, finding hidden objects, piecing together clues, and uncovering concealed mechanisms.

---

### Languages
**Category:** Knowledge | **Stat:** Intellect

Fluency in spoken and written languages beyond the common tongue. Ancient languages require this proficiency to read or speak.

> **Subspecialty — Languages (Ancient):** Reading, speaking, and interpreting pre-modern scripts and dead tongues.

---

### Lore
**Category:** Knowledge | **Stat:** Intellect

Broad recall of legends, myths, local customs, religious traditions, and folk knowledge. Less rigorous than History but wider in scope.

> **Subspecialty — Lore (Local):** Intimate knowledge of a specific region, settlement, or community — its people, politics, rumors, and geography.

---

### Medicine
**Category:** Utility | **Stat:** Intellect

Diagnosing illness, treating wounds, stabilizing the dying, and crafting antitoxins. Covers both field triage and extended care.

---

### Mining
**Category:** Knowledge / Physical | **Stat:** Prowess or Intellect

Knowledge of ore veins, tunnel construction, geological hazards, and underground navigation. Covers both the physical labor and the expertise of mining operations.

---

### Nature
**Category:** Knowledge | **Stat:** Intellect

Identifying plants, animals, weather patterns, and ecological systems. Covers wilderness biology, natural hazards, and creature behavior.

---

### Navigation
**Category:** Physical | **Stat:** Intellect

Determining location and charting routes using landmarks, stars, maps, or environmental cues. Covers both wilderness and urban wayfinding.

---

### Perception
**Category:** Physical | **Stat:** Intellect or Agility

Passive and active awareness — noticing details, detecting hidden creatures, and sensing when something is wrong. The baseline sense of the world.

---

### Performance
**Category:** Social | **Stat:** Will

Entertaining or moving an audience through music, storytelling, acting, or oratory. Covers both formal performance and informal charm.

---

### Persuasion
**Category:** Social | **Stat:** Will

Convincing others through honest appeal — logic, emotional resonance, and genuine rapport. The counterpart to Deception and Intimidation.

---

### Sleight of Hand
**Category:** Physical | **Stat:** Agility

Fine motor dexterity under pressure — pickpocketing, concealing objects, planting items, and performing subtle physical manipulations undetected.

---

### Smithing
**Category:** Craft | **Stat:** Prowess or Intellect

Forging, shaping, and finishing metal — weapons, armor, tools, and structural components. Covers both the physical craft and the metallurgical knowledge behind it.

---

### Stealth
**Category:** Physical | **Stat:** Agility

Moving without being seen or heard. Covers shadowing targets, hiding in cover, and suppressing sound and visual signature.

---

### Survival
**Category:** Physical | **Stat:** Constitution or Intellect

Enduring and navigating hostile environments — foraging, finding shelter, tracking weather, avoiding natural hazards, and staying alive without resources.

---

### Thieves' Tools
**Category:** Utility | **Stat:** Agility or Intellect

Proficiency with lockpicks, bypass tools, and other instruments of infiltration. Covers picking locks, disabling simple mechanical traps, and opening secured containers.

---

### Tracking
**Category:** Physical | **Stat:** Intellect or Agility

Following the trail left by creatures or people — footprints, scent, disturbed vegetation, and behavioral patterns. Covers both wilderness and urban tracking.

---

### Unarmed Combat
**Category:** Combat | **Stat:** Prowess

Fighting effectively without weapons — strikes, grapples, takedowns, and holds. Unarmed proficiency upgrades base damage and enables combat maneuvers.

## Skill Chains

Skill Chains are trees of named abilities purchased with Ascension Points (AP). They are the primary source of a character's active powers, techniques, and spells. Each skill in a chain has prerequisites (stat thresholds or prior skills in the chain).

Skill Chains documented so far:

### Arcane Chain
Magic-based skills covering elemental effects, area control, and arcane manipulation. Applies Primers like `[Brittle]` and `[Chilled]`.

> **Example skill:** GLACIAL BURST — Flash-freeze a 10ft zone. Enemies save vs. Agility or are pinned. Applies `[Brittle]`, `[Chilled]`. +3 Heat. Prerequisites: Will 4 / Agility 2.

### Physical Chain
Martial skills covering direct strikes, power attacks, and physical force. Detonates Primers like `[Brittle]` and `[Crush]`.

> **Example skill:** HEAVY MAUL — A devastating overhead strike. Detonates `[Shatter]`, `[Crush]`. +2 Heat. Prerequisites: Prowess 5.
