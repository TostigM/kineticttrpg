# Combat Rules

This document outlines the rules for combat in the Kinetic System.

## PART 3: THE ACTION ECONOMY

The concept of a "Turn" is defined by the physical possession and transfer of the Spotlight Token.

### Initiative

*   **The Roll:** At the start of an encounter, all participants roll Initiative.
*   **Player Advantage:** The player with the highest roll starts with the Spotlight Token.
*   **Enemy Advantage:** If the GM's Definition rolls the highest, the enemy takes one immediate "Head Start" action, and then passes the Spotlight to the highest-rolling player.

### The Player Turn (Possession)

When a player holds the Spotlight, they receive a budget of 2 Actions.

*   **Main Action (Cost: 1):** Standard attacks, heavy interactions, casting major skills.
*   **Quick Action (Cost: 0.5):** Drawing an item, shifting position, applying a minor primer. (You can take up to 4 Quick Actions if you take no Main Actions).

### The Spotlight Swap & Snap Pass

To end their turn, a player must pass the Spotlight to another player. This bypasses linear turn orders.

*   **Spotlight Swap:** Handing the token to a teammate. You may use a Quick Action to describe how you are setting them up.
*   **The Snap Pass:** To prevent "Analysis Paralysis," passing the token in under 10 seconds of real-time grants a tactical choice:
    *   The receiving player gets +1 to their next roll. OR...
    *   The global Heat Pool is reduced by 1 Tick.

## PART 4: THE HEAT ECONOMY & DEFINITIONS

There are no Monster Manuals. Every obstacle—a Dragon, an Arctic Blizzard, or a Corrupt Council—is a Definition. Definitions do not take standard "turns." Instead, they spend Heat to interrupt the players.

### Heat Generation

Every powerful action a player takes adds "Ticks" to the GM's Heat Pool.

*   **Quick Actions / Minor Moves:** +0 to +1 Heat.
*   **Core Attacks / Standard Skills:** +2 Heat.
*   **Power Moves / Heavy Spells:** +3 to +4 Heat.

### Definition Actions (GM Spending)

The GM spends accumulated Heat to trigger abilities listed on the Definition's stat sheet.

*   **Reactions (Cost: 1-2 Heat):** Standard "If/Then" triggers (e.g., "If a player moves away, bite them").
*   **Interrupts (Cost: 2-4 Heat):** Fast actions that occur during a Spotlight Swap. It does not stop the swap, but adds an immediate hazard the receiving player must deal with.
*   **Spotlight Steals (Cost: 6-10 Heat):** The GM cashes in massive Heat to seize the Spotlight Token, interrupting the player chain to take a full Enemy Turn.

### The Group Interrupt (Player Defense)

When the GM performs a Spotlight Steal, the players are not helpless. The party collectively receives 1 Group Interrupt per Enemy Turn. They must quickly decide which player uses their reaction to blunt or counter the enemy's assault.

## PART 5: THE SYNERGY LOOP (TAGS)

To eliminate math-heavy modifiers (+1s and -2s), teamwork is executed through a keyword-matching circuit called the Synergy Loop.

### Primers (Tags Appliable)

Skills that apply status effects to a target, creating a vulnerability.

*   **Example Tags:** [Brittle], [Chilled], [Exposed], [Bleeding], [Dazed].
*   **Mechanic:** A Primer does nothing immediately (or causes minor passive effects) until Detonated.

### Detonators (Tags Detonatable)

Skills that trigger specific Primers for massive, automatic effects.

*   **Example Tags:** [Shatter], [Gouge], [Crush], [Fear].
*   **Mechanic:** If a player hits a target with a Detonator that matches the target's Primer (e.g., hitting a [Brittle] target with a [Shatter] skill), it triggers an "Impactful Hit." This bypasses armor/defense and yields maximum damage or narrative success.

### Multifunctional Tags

Tags like [Force] or [Unstable] that can act as either a Primer for a complex chain or a Detonator for a basic one.

## PART 8: SKILL ANATOMY

Every ability, spell, or martial maneuver is a "Skill" formatted identically for easy reading at the table.

### Example Skill Layout:

**GLACIAL BURST (Arcane Chain)**

*   **Prerequisites:** Will 4 / Agility 2
*   **Action Cost:** 1 Main Action
*   **Effect:** Flash-freeze a 10ft zone. Enemies save vs. Agility or are pinned to the floor.
*   **Primers Applied:** [Brittle], [Chilled]
*   **Detonates:** None
*   **Heat Generation:** +3 Ticks

**HEAVY MAUL (Physical Chain)**

*   **Prerequisites:** Prowess 5
*   **Action Cost:** 1 Main Action
*   **Effect:** A devastating overhead physical strike.
*   **Primers Applied:** None
*   **Detonates:** [Shatter], [Crush]
*   **Heat Generation:** +2 Ticks
