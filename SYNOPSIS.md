### Synopsis of the Kinetic TTRPG System

The Kinetic System is a tabletop roleplaying game designed for tactical, cooperative gameplay with streamlined depth, aiming to minimize "math drag" and maximize player engagement.

**Core Mechanics:**

*   **Resolution System (3d8):** Player actions are resolved by rolling 3d8 and adding a relevant stat modifier. This creates a bell-curve probability distribution, making character specialization feel reliable and competent. The base difficulty for an average challenge is 13.

*   **Action Economy (The Spotlight System):** The game uses a dynamic "Spotlight" token to manage turns, eliminating a static initiative order. A player holding the token has a budget of 2 actions (Main or Quick) before they must pass the Spotlight to another player. Passing the token quickly can grant tactical bonuses.

*   **The Heat Economy:** Enemies, known as "Definitions," don't take traditional turns. Instead, player actions generate "Heat" for the Game Master (GM). The GM spends this Heat to have Definitions react, interrupt players, or even "steal" the Spotlight to take a full turn.

*   **The Synergy Loop (Primers & Detonators):** Teamwork is encouraged through a tag-based system. Some abilities "Prime" a target with a status (e.g., `[Brittle]`), while others "Detonate" those Primers, causing massive effects without needing complex mathematical modifiers. This makes synergy both easy to track and highly effective.

*   **Unified Pillars of Play:** The core mechanics of Spotlight, Heat, and Definitions are applied universally across all aspects of the game, including Combat, Social encounters, Exploration challenges, and Downtime projects. This creates a consistent and easy-to-learn ruleset.
